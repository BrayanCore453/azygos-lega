Privacy Policy

Azygos n8n is a private automation used only by its owner. It accesses Google Sheets only in the owner's own Google account, to read and write the rows of one spreadsheet.

The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties, and it stores no data outside the owner's own systems. Google user data is used only to operate this automation.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
